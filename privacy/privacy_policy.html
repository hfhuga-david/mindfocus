MindFocus - Privacy Policy

Protecting your privacy is our top priority

English 中文

1. 概述

MindFocus（以下简称"本应用"）是一款注意力与听觉能力训练应用。我们深知隐私保护的重要性。本隐私政策详细说明了我们如何收集、使用和保护您的信息。

核心承诺：本应用不会收集、存储或传输任何个人身份信息（包括姓名、邮箱、设备ID等）。所有训练数据均存储在您的本地设备上。

2. 我们收集的信息

2.1 本地存储数据

以下数据仅存储在您的设备上，不会上传至任何服务器：

  • 游戏进度和训练记录
  • 得分和测评结果
  • 能力评估报告
  • 应用设置和偏好配置

2.2 不收集的信息

我们明确不收集以下信息：

  • 姓名、邮箱、电话号码等个人身份信息
  • 设备标识符（IMEI、广告ID等）
  • 位置信息
  • 通讯录、相册等系统数据

3. 第三方服务说明

3.1 Edge TTS 语音合成服务

本应用使用微软 Edge TTS 技术提供语音朗读功能。当您使用听写、跟读、天气预报等需要语音输出的训练功能时，训练文本会被发送至微软的 Edge TTS 服务器进行语音合成处理。

项目 说明
发送内容 仅训练文本内容（如数字、词语、句子等）
包含个人信息 否。发送的文本不含任何个人身份信息
服务提供方 Microsoft Corporation
隐私政策 https://privacy.microsoft.com/

我们承诺：Edge TTS 服务仅用于语音合成目的，不会将训练数据与任何用户身份或设备标识关联。

3.2 Google Play 服务

本应用通过 Google Play 分发和更新。Google 可能会根据其服务条款收集相关数据。Google 的隐私政策请参阅：https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. 数据使用方式

本地存储的数据仅用于以下目的：

  • 记录和展示训练进度与得分
  • 生成个性化的能力评估报告
  • 提供连续的训练体验（如断点续练）
  • 根据历史表现调整训练难度

5. 数据安全

我们采取以下措施保护数据安全：

  • 所有数据存储在手机设备本地
  • 不通过网络传输任何个人身份信息
  • 用户可随时查看和清除所有本地数据
  • 不向任何第三方共享或出售用户数据

6. 数据删除

您可以通过以下方式删除所有数据：

  • 在应用内：进入"设置"页面，点击"清除所有数据"
  • 在设备层面：卸载本应用将自动删除所有本地数据

7. 本政策的变更

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。任何变更都会在应用内通过弹窗通知，并在此页面更新生效日期。建议在每次使用应用时仔细阅读隐私政策。

8. 联系我们

如您对本隐私政策有任何疑问、意见或建议，或希望行使数据访问、更正、删除等权利，请通过应用内设置中的联系方式与我们取得联系。

1. Overview

MindFocus (the "App") is an attention and auditory training application. We understand the importance of privacy protection. This Privacy Policy explains in detail how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Core Commitment: This App does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (including names, email addresses, device IDs, etc.). All training data is stored locally on your device.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Locally Stored Data

The following data is stored only on your device and is not uploaded to any server:

  • Game progress and training records
  • Scores and assessment results
  • Ability assessment reports
  • App settings and preferences

2.2 Information We Do NOT Collect

We explicitly do not collect the following:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personally identifiable information
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, advertising ID, etc.)
  • Location information
  • System data such as contacts or photos

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 Edge TTS Speech Synthesis Service

The App uses Microsoft Edge TTS technology to provide voice narration. When you use training features that require text-to-speech output (such as dictation, repeat-after-me, weather reports, etc.), training text is sent to Microsoft's Edge TTS server for speech synthesis processing.

Item Description
Content Sent Only training text content (e.g., numbers, words, sentences)
Contains Personal Info No. The sent text does not contain any personally identifiable information
Service Provider Microsoft Corporation
Privacy Policy https://privacy.microsoft.com/

We assure you: the Edge TTS service is used solely for speech synthesis purposes and will not associate training data with any user identity or device identifier.

3.2 Google Play Services

The App is distributed and updated through Google Play. Google may collect data according to its terms of service. For Google's privacy policy, please visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. How We Use Data

Locally stored data is used only for the following purposes:

  • Recording and displaying training progress and scores
  • Generating personalized ability assessment reports
  • Providing continuous training experience (e.g., resuming from breakpoints)
  • Adjusting training difficulty based on historical performance

5. Data Security

We take the following measures to protect data security:

  • All data is stored locally on your mobile device
  • No personally identifiable information is transmitted over the network
  • Users can view and clear all local data at any time
  • We do not share or sell user data to any third party

6. Data Deletion

You can delete all data through the following methods:

  • In the App: Go to "Settings" and tap "Clear All Data"
  • At the device level: Uninstalling the App will automatically delete all local data

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be notified through an in-app pop-up and the effective date will be updated on this page. We recommend reading the Privacy Policy carefully each time you use the App.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions, comments, or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, or wish to exercise your rights to access, correct, or delete data, please contact us through the contact information provided in the App settings.
